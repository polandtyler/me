## **Principles**

### How over What
The process by which you build a thing is just as (if not **more**) -- important than the thing you build. My preference is to reduce problems down to [first principles](https://untools.co/first-principles/). 

To that end, I have reduced my "How" down to two main principles.
1. Build in the open - collaborate early, share often, invite humility.
2. Maintain curiosity - take risks, ask questions, hang out with the experts.

### Consider the Context
We are mutable beings. 

How you and I behave in any given situation is highly specific to that scenario's context, as well as the version of ourselves that we bring to that context. 

With that disclaimer, I will try my best to outline how I behave at the extremes:

***At my best, I...***
- seek to be transparent and vulnerable
- am thoughtful and deliberate
- ask a lot of open-ended questions
- am comfortable challenging accepted norms
- use _a lot_ of emojis 😅

***At my worst, I...***
- feel like an imposter
- have a hard time focusing
- speak quickly
- also use _a lot_ of emojis 🤷‍♂️


## How I operate (tl;dr):

**My preferred communication method** is asynchronous - i.e. IM or email.

**"Flow" times are important, but so are you.**  I block out large swaths of my day to focusing on development, but in reality my work flows in 60-90 minute [ultradian rhythms](https://www.fastcompany.com/3013188/why-you-need-to-unplug-every-90-minutes) - so feel free to message me and I will get back to you within 90 minutes (usually sooner, because you come first).


## Quirks
Like you, I also have some. They are:

#### **I frown when I'm thinking**  
If you see me with a disconcerted look on my face, it probably means that I am puzzling something out internally and not that I am upset. Joyful is my default state, I promise. 🙂

#### **Transparency is King** 👑
In software (and life), hidden states create inefficiencies. I have yet to be convinced that overcommunication. I will take open, awkward "TMI" moments over veiled pleasantries every day of the week (and twice on Sunday).

#### **Growth through opposition** 
Experience has taught me that _respectful_ argument is one of the best innovation tools we have at our disposal. To this end, I often find myself playing the role of devil's advocate. If you present an idea and I challenge you, please do not take it personally. There's a very good chance that I actually agree with you.
